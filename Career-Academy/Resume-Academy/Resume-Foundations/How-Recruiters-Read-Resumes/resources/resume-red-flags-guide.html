Resume Red Flags Guide

Red FlagWhy It HurtsStronger Alternative
Dense paragraphsSlow to scan and hide evidence.Use concise bullets and clear spacing.
Generic summaryDoes not establish relevance.Use target-role strengths and proof points.
No metricsPerformance and scope remain unclear.Add volume, quality, time, dollars, percentages, or scale.
Inconsistent datesCreates credibility concerns.Verify dates and titles across all materials.
Irrelevant detailsDistract from target-role fit.Prioritize relevant evidence.
Keyword stuffingWeakens readability and credibility.Use keywords naturally and prove them in experience.
