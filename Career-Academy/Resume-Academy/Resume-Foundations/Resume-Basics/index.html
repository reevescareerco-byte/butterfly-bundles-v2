Activity 2 of 4

Resume vs. Job Application

Review the difference before continuing.

Progress40%
XP50

Resume

Purpose: Market relevant qualifications.
Content: Selected experience, achievements, skills, and education.
Strategy: Tailored to the target role.
Format: Designed for readability and impact.

Job Application

Purpose: Collect standardized employment information.
Content: Complete dates, employers, duties, and required disclosures.
Strategy: Answer every required field accurately.
Format: Controlled by the employer’s system.
Key distinction:
A resume is a targeted marketing document. A job application is a formal employer record. The information must remain consistent across both.

Key Takeaways
A resume is a targeted marketing document designed to earn the next step. Choose a format that supports your career story, keep information consistent with applications, and gather dates, tools, metrics, accomplishments, education, and certifications before writing.
