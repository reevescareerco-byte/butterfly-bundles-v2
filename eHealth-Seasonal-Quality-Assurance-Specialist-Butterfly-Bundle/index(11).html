Coaching Workshop™

Write feedback that is specific, respectful, and behavior-based.

Agent response: “I do not understand why I lost points. The customer still enrolled.”

Expert response: “The enrollment outcome was positive, but the deduction relates to the unsupported statement that the plan would cover everything. On future calls, verify the medication list and explain coverage using approved language before recommending enrollment. That protects the customer and keeps the call aligned with quality standards.”
