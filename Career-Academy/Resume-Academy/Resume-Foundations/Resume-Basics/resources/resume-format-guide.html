Resume Format Comparison Guide

FormatHow It WorksBest ForUse Cautiously When
Reverse ChronologicalLists experience from most recent backward.Steady work history, similar target roles, visible career progression.Large gaps or frequent short roles require careful explanation.
CombinationLeads with qualifications or skills, then shows chronological work history.Career changes, transferable skills, diverse experience.Skills and experience sections become repetitive.
FunctionalEmphasizes skill categories more than dates and employers.Unusual work histories, project-based experience, severe gaps.Recruiters or ATS expect clear chronology. Use only with a strong reason.
General recommendation: Reverse chronological is appropriate for most candidates. Combination is often the strongest alternative for career changers. Functional formats should be used selectively.
