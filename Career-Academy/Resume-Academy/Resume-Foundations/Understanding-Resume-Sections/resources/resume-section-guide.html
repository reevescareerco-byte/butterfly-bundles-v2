Resume Section Guide

SectionPurposeIncludeAvoid
Contact InformationIdentify and contact the candidate.Name, city/state, phone, professional email, LinkedIn.Full address, unprofessional email, personal details.
Targeted HeadlineCommunicate professional direction.Target role or specialty.Generic labels and unrelated titles.
Professional SummaryIntroduce relevant experience and value.Experience, strengths, proof points.Generic traits and long paragraphs.
Core CompetenciesSurface role-specific skills and keywords.Job-specific capabilities and tools.Keyword stuffing and irrelevant skills.
Professional ExperienceProve performance and impact.Employers, titles, dates, achievements, metrics.Copied job descriptions and inconsistent dates.
EducationShow academic preparation.Degree/diploma, school, expected date when current.Elementary education and unnecessary dates.
CertificationsShow licenses and credentials.Name, issuer, expiration when relevant.Expired or unrelated credentials.
