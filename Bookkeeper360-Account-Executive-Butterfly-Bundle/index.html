Sample Cover Letter

Dear Bookkeeper360 Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Account Executive position because it brings together the areas where I have produced the strongest results: consultative B2B selling, executive-level discovery, financial and workforce solutions, and full-cycle ownership from qualified lead through signed agreement.

In my current role, I manage a $2.4 million annual quota across accounting automation, payroll, and advisory services for small and mid-sized businesses. I finished the past two years at 118% and 111% of quota, maintain a 33% win rate, and manage more than 40 active opportunities each month. My approach centers on understanding how a business operates, where financial or administrative friction exists, and what outcome the owner is trying to achieve before recommending a solution.

I am particularly interested in Bookkeeper360 because of its ability to combine accounting, payroll, tax, HR, and advisory support in one technology-enabled client experience. That breadth creates an opportunity for a true consultative seller to diagnose multiple business needs, develop a tailored recommendation, and build long-term trust with business owners.

I would bring disciplined pipeline management, 97% forecast accuracy, strong HubSpot and PandaDoc experience, and a consistent record of converting complex business challenges into closed revenue. I would also prioritize a clean transition to onboarding so that expectations established during the sales process carry through to the client experience.

Thank you for your consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience exceeding quota and selling financial and professional services can support Bookkeeper360’s continued growth.

Sincerely,
Morgan Reed

Objection Handling Lab™

Prospect objection: “Your solution costs more than the local bookkeeper we already use.”

Strong response: “That makes sense, and I would expect you to compare the investment. The difference is that you are not only evaluating bookkeeping. You mentioned limited visibility, separate payroll, tax stress, and uncertainty around hiring. Before we compare price directly, would it be helpful to quantify what those gaps are costing you in time, risk, and missed decision-making?”
