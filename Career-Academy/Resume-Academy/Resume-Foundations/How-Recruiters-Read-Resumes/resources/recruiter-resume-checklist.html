Recruiter Resume Checklist

Target role or professional direction is immediately clear.
Professional summary establishes relevant experience and value.
Recent job titles and dates are easy to find.
Role-specific skills and tools are visible.
Experience bullets provide measurable evidence.
Formatting supports fast scanning.
Dates, titles, and employers are consistent.
Irrelevant information does not distract from fit.
Keywords are supported by experience.
Education and credentials support qualification.
