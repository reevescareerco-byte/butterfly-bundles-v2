Professor Reeva's Briefing

Heartbeat Health is not hiring someone to simply answer phones. They are hiring the person who keeps patients connected to care.

Your value is your ability to reach patients, reduce confusion, solve small barriers before they become missed appointments, document accurately, and create a friction-free experience across phone, text, email, virtual visits, and diagnostic scheduling.

Your winning message: “I combine high-touch service, organized follow-through, and technology confidence to help patients complete the next step in their care.”

Sample Cover Letter

Dear Heartbeat Health Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Patient Experience Coordinator position because it combines the areas where I add the most value: high-touch service, proactive follow-up, technology support, and mission-driven work. My background in fast-paced customer-facing environments has prepared me to communicate with empathy, solve problems calmly, and keep people moving toward the next step even when they are frustrated, uncertain, or unfamiliar with a process.

In my current customer experience role, I manage more than 60 daily interactions across phone, email, and text, coordinate appointments, resolve scheduling issues, and guide customers through digital platforms. By strengthening confirmations and follow-up, I helped reduce missed appointments by 17%. I also resolve 88% of routine technology questions during the first interaction and maintain a 95% quality score through accurate documentation and consistent service.

I am especially drawn to Heartbeat Health's virtual-first approach to cardiovascular care. The opportunity to help patients schedule critical visits and diagnostics, navigate technology, and remain engaged in their heart health is meaningful to me. I would bring strong organization, clear communication, comfort with new systems, and a service-first mindset to every patient interaction.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to a team that is expanding access to high-quality heart care while improving the patient experience through technology and thoughtful coordination. Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
Jordan Taylor
