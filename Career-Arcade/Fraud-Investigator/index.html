Step-by-Step Instructions

Exactly How to Play

Each case has evidence cards and four investigation zones. Complete these steps in order.

1
Read the entire case summary.

Review the provider, member, claim pattern, documentation, financial amount, and timing before moving any evidence.

2
Drag every evidence card into a zone.

Move each card into Clear, Monitor, Investigate, or Escalate. On a phone or tablet, tap an evidence card and then tap the destination zone.

3
Use the zone meanings correctly.

Clear means normal or explained. Monitor means not enough for formal action but worth watching. Investigate means evidence supports a formal review. Escalate means high risk, urgent exposure, or specialized authority is needed.

4
Choose one final case decision.

After all evidence is placed, select the overall outcome. The game will compare both your evidence sorting and final decision with the correct investigation logic.

Example: A normal billing increase caused by a new office location may belong in Clear. Identical notes across dozens of claims may belong in Investigate. A multimillion-dollar exposure involving multiple providers may require Escalate.
