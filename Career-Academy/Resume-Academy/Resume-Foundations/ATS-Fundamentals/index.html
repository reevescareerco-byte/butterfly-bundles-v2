Section 1 of 6

What Is an ATS?

An ATS is mainly a hiring-management platform, not a universal robot that independently chooses every candidate.

Progress15%

Application database

Stores resumes, profiles, answers, notes, communications, and hiring status.

Workflow manager

Helps recruiters move candidates through review, interviews, offers, holds, and rejection.

Search and filter tool

May support searches by skills, titles, employers, credentials, location, or application answers.

Parsing engine

Extracts text and attempts to classify experience, skills, education, and contact information.

Important: ATS products and employer settings vary. There is no single universal ATS score or one identical filtering method.
