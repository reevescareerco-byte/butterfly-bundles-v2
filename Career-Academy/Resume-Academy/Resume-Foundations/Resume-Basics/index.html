1985

Paper resume

Career Record

Chronology carried directly to employers.

Click to open this exhibit →

What recruiters saw: Employers, job titles, dates, and responsibilities on a physical page.

What stayed the same: The employer still needed quick proof that the candidate could do the work.

2005

Online application

Searchable Data

Stored, parsed, filtered, and reviewed.

Click to open this exhibit →

What changed: Applicant tracking systems could search resumes for titles, skills, education, and keywords.

The lesson: Clear sections and recognizable language became essential for both software and people.

Today

Targeted career marketing

Relevant Evidence

Built for one opportunity and one next step.

Click to open this exhibit →

What wins attention: Relevant skills, visible outcomes, credible numbers, and alignment with the target role.

The lesson: A strong resume does not include everything. It prioritizes the evidence that matters now.
